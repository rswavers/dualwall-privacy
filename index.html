DualWall Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-12 · App: DualWall (com.raseraser.dualwall)

Summary

DualWall does not collect, store on any server, sell or share your personal data. It has no ads, no accounts and no analytics or tracking SDKs. Your photos and videos stay on your device.

Photos and videos you choose

When you pick a photo or video, DualWall copies it into the app's private storage on your device and may re-encode it (resize, remove audio) so it plays smoothly as a wallpaper. This happens entirely on your device. The files are never uploaded and are deleted when you remove them in the app or uninstall it. They are excluded from cloud backups.

Settings and battery information

Your wallpaper and charging-animation settings, and battery readings used to draw the charging percentage, are stored and used only on your device.

Purchases

DualWall Pro and tips are sold through Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google, not by DualWall. The app receives from Google Play only what it needs to confirm a purchase (such as the product and a purchase token) and keeps a "Pro unlocked" flag on your device. Google's privacy policy applies to purchases: policies.google.com/privacy.

Third-party components

The app uses the Google Play Billing Library to process purchases. That library communicates with Google Play and may send diagnostic information to Google; this is why the app declares network permissions. DualWall itself sends no data to the developer or to any other party.

Permissions

  • Set wallpaper: to apply your photos as wallpapers.
  • Internet / network state / billing: used by the Google Play Billing and media playback libraries.

Children

DualWall is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: rswavers@gmail.com.

DualWall 隱私權政策

生效日期 2026-10-12 · App：DualWall（com.raseraser.dualwall）

摘要

DualWall 不會收集、上傳到任何伺服器、出售或分享你的個人資料。沒有廣告、不需帳號，也不含分析或追蹤 SDK。你的照片和影片只留在你的裝置上。

你選擇的照片和影片

你選擇照片或影片時，DualWall 會把它複製到 App 在裝置上的私人儲存空間，並可能重新編碼（縮小尺寸、移除聲音）以便順暢地作為桌布播放。整個過程都在你的裝置上完成，檔案不會上傳；在 App 中移除或解除安裝 App 時會刪除，也不會包含在雲端備份中。

設定與電池資訊

桌布與充電動畫設定，以及用來顯示充電百分比的電池讀數，只在你的裝置上儲存與使用。

購買

DualWall Pro 與贊助透過 Google Play 販售，付款資料由 Google 處理，DualWall 無法取得。App 只會從 Google Play 收到確認購買所需的資訊（例如商品與購買憑證），並在裝置上記錄「Pro 已解鎖」。購買相關事項適用 Google 隱私權政策：policies.google.com/privacy。

第三方元件

App 使用 Google Play 帳款服務程式庫（Billing Library）處理購買。該程式庫會與 Google Play 通訊，並可能傳送診斷資訊給 Google，這也是 App 宣告網路權限的原因。DualWall 本身不會傳送任何資料給開發者或其他第三方。

權限

  • 設定桌布：將你的照片設為桌布。
  • 網路／網路狀態／帳款服務：供 Google Play 帳款服務與媒體播放程式庫使用。

兒童

DualWall 並非以 13 歲以下兒童為對象，也不會蓄意收集任何人的資訊。

變更與聯絡方式

本政策如有變更，新版本會公布在此頁面並更新生效日期。如有疑問請來信：rswavers@gmail.com。
